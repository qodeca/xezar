11. Hover, safe areas and the keyboard

  • no-hover: is a custom variant for @media (hover: none). A control that only appears on hover MUST also be visible under no-hover:, or a phone can never reach it.
  • The shell pads with env(safe-area-inset-*) on phones. Heights use dvh, never 100vh, so the on-screen keyboard does not hide the composer.
  • Focus is :focus-visible only: a 3 px ring in --ring at 50 %. Press Tab on this page to see it on the bar controls.
