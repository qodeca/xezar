### 2026-09-20 — A red proof that stayed green, and the break that was the wrong one

- RP-2.4 ("append the leader's prompt inside the numbered list instead of after it") went GREEN on
  the first attempt, and the test was not at fault: the break itself was a no-op. Splicing the
  prompt into `blocks` at index 1 appends it when the brief has one block, which is exactly the
  case the test exercises — so the broken renderer produced byte-identical output. Only a break
  that interleaves the prompt among the numbered LINES made it red. The lesson is the one
  AGENTS.md § Changing a mechanism already states from the other side: a red proof measures the
  break as much as the test, and "the test passed against the break" is two hypotheses, not one.
- Assumption A4 of the spec (is the reviewing task's resolved backend and model readable when the
  tool runs?) held, and reading the resolution path first changed the design rather than confirming
  it: `RunRecord.model` is frequently ABSENT, so comparing raw recorded values would have let the
  independence guard fail open on the commonest case. Resolving both sides through the same
  per-runner default is what makes "neither side named a model" compare equal instead of comparing
  two unknowns.
