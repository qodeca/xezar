### 2026-09-23 — A child `close` event does not prove its process group is empty

- A timeout sent SIGTERM to the saved provider-process group, then the group leader's `close`
  handler cleared the pending SIGKILL timer. A surviving grandchild could therefore outlive the
  completed quota check even though the direct child was gone.
- Full-path process tests should save the spawned PGID, verify the required signal reached that
  exact group, and assert the group is empty afterward. Direct-child liveness and command-pattern
  searches do not establish the process-group boundary.
