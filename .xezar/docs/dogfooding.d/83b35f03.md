### 2026-09-20 — A badge that contradicted the two lines under it, and where a policy window belongs

- **A state word that means "one of four different things" will eventually contradict the screen.**
  `unknown` covered no-clone, never-fetched, diverged AND a check older than six hours, and the
  fourth case printed two known versions under a badge reading "Version unknown". Two code reviews
  and a QA pass all read that as correct — because it IS correct as a comparison result. Only the
  design review, reading the words as a person would, saw the contradiction. A verdict enum is UI
  copy the moment a surface renders it verbatim; splitting `stale-check` out of `unknown` in the
  zod schema cost one line and removed the contradiction at the source rather than in the renderer.
- **Where a policy window lives decides how many places can disagree about it.** The fix could have
  been a UI-side "is `fetchedAt` older than six hours" test. That would have put the passive-fetch
  window in two files, and the MCP door — which relays the same block to a leader — would still have
  said `unknown`. Keeping the six hours in `compareState` and letting the state word travel means
  both doors say the same thing and neither re-derives the rule.
- **A `refetchInterval` added "only for the cold-boot race" outlives the race.** The catalog poll
  was bounded by `state === 'unknown'`, and an unreachable source is `unknown` forever — so the
  bound was not a bound. Its replacement is a pure, exported function with an attempt cap, which is
  also the only reason the "poll continues past the bound" red proof could be written at all: a
  predicate inlined in a hook option has no seam to prove anything against.
