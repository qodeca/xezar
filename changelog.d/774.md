## 🐛 Fixes

- 🐛 **A Settings section no longer shifts when its data arrives.** The twelve Settings sections
  that show their own "Loading …" line padded it with a hand-set 24 px gutter, while the panel that
  replaced it a moment later used the design system's own section spacing – so the text jumped as
  soon as the section loaded. Both states now share one spacing, at every density, and the
  Bookmarklets panel moves onto the same pair. (#774)
