Decisions – UI design

Some choices are the owner’s: new scope, what “done” means, a breaking change, publishing or releasing. Today nothing stops an agent or the leader from making them, and nothing shows the owner that one was made. This design gives those choices one place, stops the task and frees its slot until the owner answers, and keeps the leader’s hands off the answer.

Design decisions

DecisionChoiceWhy
NameDecisionsOne word, like every other menu item. Says what you do there. The owner picked it over “Human in the loop” on 2026-09-14.
Menu placeTasks, Decisions, Inbox, Git…Second, because it holds the things that stop work. Inbox holds ideas that stop nothing.
Always onNo gate, no settingA gate the leader may not clear cannot be a feature the owner may not have turned on. The Inbox is off by default; this is not the Inbox.
Badge colourVioletViolet in the menu already means “a person is wanted”. This is that, exactly. Screen readers hear “Decisions, 3 decisions waiting”.
IconRaised hand (lucide hand)“Stop, a person decides”. Tasks has the list-with-ticks, Inbox the tray.
Card shapeThe thread’s ask card, plus a header line, a plain-words block, collapsed technical details and a comment boxPeople already know the ask card. XEZ:ASK already copies Claude Code’s AskUserQuestion one to one, and the owner asked for that model.
Category chipA word on the muted surface, not the accentThree accent chips beside a primary Send would invert “lime is the one call to action”; under the violet accent the accent chip fails contrast at 10 px. The word carries the category.
SendThe primary (lime) button, as on the ask cardOne call to action per card; “Not a decision” is a ghost beside it.
“Recommended”A word in the option label, muted, naming who recommendsColour is never the only signal. “Recommended by the agent” says whose opinion it is.
Two descriptionsPlain words always visible, body size; technical details collapsedThe owner reads a case without having read the thread: what was being done, what happened, what they decide. The plain text is required; xezar writes one when the raiser did not. Owner’s requirement, 2026-09-14.
GitHub linksThe task’s issue and PR on the header line, opening GitHub in a new tabThe widest context is one click away: the issue says what was asked, the PR shows what was done. Read from the run’s refs; hidden when the task has none. Owner’s requirement, 2026-09-14 evening.
EvidenceInside “Technical details”, collapsedThe question and the plain words must be read first. A diff stat or an issue quote is there when you want to check.
Four answersAn option, a comment, “Not a decision”, or “Leader decides”“Not a decision” closes the case, lets the task carry on and counts the false alarm. “Leader decides” hands one case to the leader: it leaves the owner’s badge, the task stays stopped, the leader answers with a required reason, the owner can take it back. Both counted, so the owner can watch whether the gate over-asks or wears away. Owner’s addition, 2026-09-14 evening.
Taking it backThe send hint says whether the answer can be taken backReopen works until the task acts on the decision; “Publish” says it cannot be taken back once the task acts.
On the task pageA Decisions tab beside Session, Changes and Files, plus the card in the threadThe thread scrolls; the tab is the task’s decision history, one list, open first then “Decided · n”. Owner’s requirement, 2026-09-14.
Who can answerYou, in the cockpit; or the leader, quoting your wordsYou talk to the leader, not to xezar, so most answers will arrive through it. xezar shows the relayed words and makes Reopen one click. The owner chose this over MCP elicitation for now.
Task statusA new run state, decisionA stopped task holds no slot, no session, no timer, and goes back to the queue on the answer. waiting could not do that: its idle timer, restart recovery, the autonomous nudge and auto-resume all leave it. Owner’s call in the interview (D-12).
Coming backResume with the agent’s context; fresh agent for the same step when that failsThe owner asked for the context the agent had before it asked. The decided card says which path ran.
Who opens a caseA task agent (XEZ:DECIDE), the leader, or xezar’s own brief check when a leader creates or edits a taskThe brief check is the net that catches the motivating case: the leader’s brief named a control the issue did not. The diff check after the work is report-only in version 1.
CategoriesScope · Done means · Breaking change · Outside action (publish or release only)Fixed in version 1. Spend stays with the leader. Owner narrowed “outside action” in the interview.
ComposerDisabled while a case is open on that taskToday any message resolves an agent’s question. Here that would let the task slip past the gate.
The InboxStays separate, unchangedTwo jobs, two places: optional ideas that stop nothing, and decisions that stop work. Owner’s choice, 2026-09-14.

Accessibility checklist for the build

  • Badge: the number is hidden from screen readers and replaced with words: “Decisions, 3 decisions waiting”. The mobile bar’s count carries the same words.
  • Nothing is colour alone: the amber dot has “needs your decision” beside it, the category is a word, “Recommended” is a word.
  • One sr-only role="status" line per surface announces a case that opens or is decided. The cards are not a live region; the banner is not a live region.
  • Option buttons carry aria-pressed; the group is role="group" labelled by the question. Technical details is a native <details>, so it opens from the keyboard without script.
  • A disabled Send has its reason as visible text beside it, in the muted colour, never only in a tooltip.
  • After Send, focus moves to the next open card or to the page heading; the typed comment is kept when a race refuses the answer.
  • Motion (pulse, skeleton) stops under “reduce motion”.
  • Every action works from the keyboard and shows the :focus-visible ring; the comment box has a visually hidden label.
