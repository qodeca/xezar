States

Every state the menu badge, a case card and the whole page can be in. Each state says what happened and what to do next. No state lets an agent, an automation or the leader close a case on its own.

1. Menu badge

The number is the count of open cases in this project. It is read from the run snapshots the workspace stream already carries (each case lives on its run record), so it changes the moment a case opens or is decided. There is no separate query for it.

Nothing open – no badge
Tasks2 Decisions Inbox

Zero hides the badge. Silence means nothing is waiting on you. The item itself is always there, so a first-time user learns where decisions will appear.

One, many, and the cap

Violet, like Inbox and Tasks. Violet in the menu already means “a person is wanted”, and that is exactly what a case is. Screen readers hear “Decisions, 7 decisions waiting”.

Several projects in the sidebar

Per project, plus a count on a collapsed group. A collapsed project that has an open case shows the number on its header, the same way it shows “tasks need you” today. One workspace-level read, subscribed once at the root, feeds every count. A decision you cannot see is a task that waits for ever.

2. Case card

One card per case. The first line says the state, the category, who raised it, how long it has waited and which task is stopped. The question is the biggest text. The plain-words text is always visible. The technical reason and the evidence are one click below.

    The agent is tidying the list of tool names. One old name, claude-cli, is still accepted so that very old task records keep opening. The agent would like to stop accepting it. If it does, records from before version 0.9 stop loading. You decide whether to keep them loading or to plan the removal for a later major version.

    Technical details

    Why this is yours: run files written before 0.9 would stop loading. BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md § 1 calls that a break.

    The agent’s reasons

    The id has been an alias for 14 releases. Keeping it costs one line in the schema and one test. Dropping it lets the runner map be a strict record.

    Pick an option, write a comment, or both. You can take it back until the task acts on it.

Send is disabled until there is something to send. An option alone, a comment alone, or both. A comment alone means “none of these – read my words”, which is how the thread’s ask card already treats a free-text reply. The hint is the visible reason Send is disabled, and it says whether the answer can be taken back. Two more answers sit beside Send: “Not a decision” closes the case, the task continues as if it had never opened, and the false alarm is counted. “Leader decides” hands this one case to the leader (state i below). Both are yours alone; the leader cannot give either.

    Technical details

    Why this is yours: the leader’s brief adds a cockpit control that the linked issue does not name.

    Sends “Move it to a new issue” and your comment to the task. You can take it back until the task acts on it.

The hint says exactly what will be sent. The picked option gets the ask card’s tinted border. Sending replaces the label with “Sending…” and makes the card inert. When the card leaves the Open list, focus moves to the next open card, or to the page heading when it was the last.

c · Decided by you
  • Decided —Move it to a new issue· by you · 14:32 #403 codex wake path issue #374, opens GitHub in a new tab
    Keep the wake path. The control needs its own issue with a design first.
    The task went back to the queue and resumed with its context at 14:35.

One line, then your words. The card leaves the Open segment and appears under Decided with the same shape. The foot says how the task came back: “resumed with its context” (the agent’s session continued) or “re-ran the step with a fresh agent” (the session could not be resumed). Reopen puts the case back in Open and stops the task again; the agent is told at its next turn boundary.

d · Decided through the leader
  • Decided —Publish· passed on by the leader · 09:05 releasing 0.15.0
    Owner said: “go ahead with the release, npm and the GitHub Release, and merge the bump PR.”
    The leader recorded these words as yours. If they are not, reopen the case.

The leader’s relay is visible, quoted and reversible. Most of your decisions will reach xezar this way, because you talk to the leader, not to xezar. xezar cannot check that the words are yours, so it shows them and makes reopening one click.

e · Reopened
  • needs your decision Outside action raised by the leader · reopened by you 1m releasing 0.15.0

    Publish 0.15.0 to npm now?

    In plain words

    The release task has prepared version 0.15.0. The next step sends the package to npm and cannot be taken back. You had said “Publish” through the leader, then took it back before anything was sent. You decide again.

    Technical details

    Why this is yours: publishing leaves this machine and cannot be undone.

    The earlier decision

    09:05 — “Publish”, passed on by the leader: “go ahead with the release…”. Withdrawn by you at 09:06. The task had not acted on it.

    Pick an option, write a comment, or both. “Publish” cannot be taken back once the task acts.

Reopen works only while nothing has acted. The engine sets “acted on” at the first outward effect after the decision – the next agent turn on the run, or a push, PR, ready or merge on its branch – never from a tool argument. After that the Reopen button is gone and the foot reads “Acted on at 09:07 — cannot be reopened.” While an agent turn is in flight, Reopen is refused with that reason and the button shows it.

f · Closed without a decision
  • No longer needed· the task was cancelled · 03:12 #369 pi approveTools guidance
    Nobody decided this. The question is kept for the record.

A cancelled or deleted task closes its case. The card moves to Decided with the words “No longer needed”, never with a decision that nobody made.

g · Not a decision
  • Not a decision· by you · 08:41 #418 flaky teardown tests
    Renaming a test helper is not a breaking change. Carry on.
    The task continued as if the case had not opened. Counted as a false alarm.

The false alarms are counted, not hidden. This is how the owner watches whether the checks and the agents over-ask. Only the cockpit can give this answer; the leader cannot.

h · Someone else answered first
This case was decided a moment ago. The leader recorded “Build it” at 14:31, while you were writing. Your answer was not sent; your comment is kept in the box. Read the decision under Decided and reopen it if it is wrong.

Two answers never race silently. The second one is refused with the first one shown – the same stale-write rule every task change already follows, guarded by the case’s own version token. The typed comment stays in the box so nothing is lost.

i · With the leader (you pressed “Leader decides”)
  • With the leader· since 14:32 · Scope #403 codex wake path
    You wrote: “Pick whichever keeps #403 small. I do not need the button.”
    The leader answers over the MCP with a reason. The task stays stopped and its slot stays free until then. Not counted in your badge.

Handed over, not closed. The case leaves your badge and the “needs you” bucket – the task’s pill reads “with the leader” – and joins the leader’s morning count. It stays under Open on this page so you can see it. Take it back returns it to you at any moment before the leader answers; you may also answer it yourself while it is with the leader. With no leader connected the button is disabled and the words “No leader is connected” sit beside it.

j · Decided by the leader after you handed it over
  • Decided —Drop it· by the leader · delegated by you at 14:32 · 14:40 #403 codex wake path
    Leader’s reason: “The owner does not need the button; #403 stays the wake path only. A note on #374 records the idea.”
    The task went back to the queue and resumed with its context at 14:41.

The leader’s own answer looks different from a relayed one. “by the leader · delegated by you” with the leader’s reason, never “Owner said”. A leader call with owner words on a delegated case is refused, so the two can never be confused in the record. Reopen works until the task acts, as always.

3. Whole page

Empty – nothing waiting

No decisions waiting

When an agent, the leader or xezar meets a choice that is yours, it appears here and in the menu.

Only you can answer these. Agents and the leader bring you the choices they may not make: new scope, what “done” means, breaking changes, and publishing or releasing. The task stops and frees its slot until you decide. The leader cannot decide for you.

The rule strip lives here only. A page with cases starts with the first card; the rule is not repeated on every visit.

Decided segment, empty

Nothing decided yet

Decisions you make, and the ones the leader passes on for you, are kept here with the words that were used.

Decided grows. The segment shows the newest 20 and a “Show older” button at the end; the task’s own Decisions tab is the place to read one task’s history.

Hosted mode
Deciding works in a hosted cockpit. A decision changes a task record, not the host machine.

Stated, not designed. The design system asks every page to say what hosted mode refuses. This page refuses nothing, so it shows no refusal state; the row above is the note, not a UI element.
