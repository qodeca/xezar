Air – appearance axes

The proposed view under each appearance axis the cockpit has: four densities on the thread and on the Settings list, dark and light with both accents, and both reading widths. Each frame pins the axes its caption names; the bar sets the rest. At the other densities every proposed value is the Comfortable value × 1.25 (Roomy), × 0.875 (Compact) or × 0.75 (Compact for real).

Density – the thread

Four 1:1 frames of the proposed thread at full width, each opened at the second speaker’s first row (#speaker-2), so a row inside a turn and the gap between two speakers’ rows are both in view. One unit is 5 px at Roomy, 4 at Comfortable, 3.5 at Compact and 3 at Compact for real (cockpit.css:182-188, Roomy in styles.css); the six rhythm values follow it.

Density – the Settings list

Settings → Appearance at 375 px, proposed, at each density: the field list, the density control itself and the phone touch targets. The mobile bar and Open menu are h-11 and size-11 (app-shell.tsx:848, 859), so they scale with the lever – proportional by D-3 (README § 10).

The density control lists Roomy · Comfortable · Compact · Compact for real. Today it is inline-flex w-fit and cannot wrap (appearance.tsx:66); four options come to about 390 px against a column of about 343 px, so the proposed row wraps inside its own border – a new requirement for PR 4 (README § 9.4).

Theme and accent

The proposed thread at 375 px in dark and light, with the lime and violet accents. Spacing is identical in all four; nothing in the proposal is theme-specific.

Dark with violet: the violet accent points --primary at violet (cockpit.css:176-180), so the send button (the default primary variant, composer.tsx:576-583, button.tsx:16, 30) and the running dot (--violet) share one colour – by design (theming.md:74). User bubbles sit on --muted (thread-items.tsx:121).

Reading width

Wide sets --measure to 1180 px; narrow is 820 (cockpit.css:106, 190-192). At a 1280 window the main column is 1016 px (1280 − 264, cockpit.css:527), under 1180, so wide only takes effect from 1444 px. These two frames are therefore a 1600 × 900 window shown at 70.75 % – the only scaled frames in the set. Their 1:1 links show the difference in a window of 1444 px or more.

Narrow

The column padding sits inside the measure (task-thread.tsx:307), so the text line is the measure less two gutters: 820 − 2 × 24 = 772 today → 820 − 2 × 32 = 756 proposed.
