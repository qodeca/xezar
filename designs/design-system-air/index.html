Air – how to review the step-0 mockup

Step 0 of qodeca/xezar#424. Each screen is the cockpit drawn twice at 1:1: Today, before #424 (read on 2026-09-14), with values from packages/web/src, and Proposed, the end state after PR 4 of the README – rhythm tokens, looser gaps between blocks, no hand-set pixels and a Roomy density. Controls keep their size; only the space between things moves. Every task, path and message on these pages is invented.

Status: implemented under #424 (closed 2026-09-15). The design-review chose A for both open points (D-11, D-12, README § 13). Today preserves the pre-#424 cockpit; the proposal shipped with remaining allowlist conversion in #445.

How to review

  1. Use a 1280 × 900 window. A screen page draws the cockpit at the window’s own width, 1:1, with its real breakpoints. Its values assume 1280 or 375 px; at any other width the bar says so.
  2. Flip in place. On a screen page, Show on the bar switches between Today and Proposed with no reload, so the scroll position holds and a 2–4 px change shows as movement. Focus a Show option and ←/→ flips back and forth; the line beside it says which view is showing.
  3. See both at once. “Compare at 1280” on each screen page opens the compare page: that page twice at 1:1, Today above and Proposed below, at the same anchor.
  4. Check the numbers. Each screen page ends with “Values on this screen”: where, today with its source file:line, and proposed as token = px at Comfortable.
  5. Look at 375 px on the pages listed under “Where 375 px is true”.
  6. Set the axes once. Theme, Accent, Density and Width on the bar reach every page and every frame, and this browser remembers them and the Show choice. Links carry them, so a click keeps the view you chose; a link that names a key keeps its own.

Scripts are required, as for the other mockups in designs/. Opened from disk, the address bar may not follow a change; the bar says so.

Open points 6 and 7

Two values README § 9.2 and § 9.3 left open. The step-0 verdict chose A for both (D-11 and D-12, README § 13). Both options still switch live on Tasks.

  • 6 – task-table wrapper: A – keeps the section gutter, 32 px · B – exempt, today’s 20 px
  • 7 – New-task button: A – 40 px · B – 36 px. At 375 px the button sits in the drawer: see the “Tasks – menu open” pair on 375 px.

Questions the verdict answered

  1. Open point 6. At 1280 the proposed table is 1389 px wide (1349 today). A shows 950 px and hides 439; B shows 974 and hides 415. Both cut IN / OUT and hide Cost, CPU, Mem and Started. Is B’s extra 24 px worth a second gutter rule? Under B the footer strip keeps the 32 px gutter while the table moves to 20, so the two sit 12 px out of line.
  2. What “exempt” means. B draws today’s 20 px gutter, an assumption; README § 9.2 says only “exempt the wrapper”, which could also mean no gutter at all. Which one?
  3. Open point 7. Should New task be 40 px (A) or 36 px (B) beside the 36 px nav rows? At phone width the drawer rows are 44 px, so the button is shorter than its rows under both options.
  4. Add project. It stays 36 px beside a 40 px New task under option A (not in § 9.2 – unchanged). Should it follow the CTA, as its source comment says (app-shell.tsx:734)?
  5. Page header x. Tasks, Inbox and Settings keep the header’s px-5 = 20 while the body gutter moves to 32, so the title sits 12 px out of line with the content. Align it?
  6. Settings section containers. p-4 md:p-6 (agents-section.tsx:163, appearance.tsx:120, accounts-section.tsx:147) is not assigned by § 9.2, so both views draw it unchanged: 24 at 1280, 16 at 375. The refusal block’s gap-4 is in the same position. Give them a rhythm token?
  7. Inside an Inbox card. The card’s inner gap-2.5 = 10 (inbox.tsx:227) stays while its padding grows 16 → 20. At 375 the proposed card narrows the summary text from 140 to 124 px, and the list grows from 1,298 to 1,477 px (722 → 804 at 1280). Keep the inner gap?
  8. Nearest-step picks. Two hand-set values fall between scale steps and are drawn at the larger one: the project-row gap 7 → 8 and the Inbox status-dot offset 5 → 6 (PR 3b decides). Accept them?
  9. The density row wraps at 375. Four options, about 390 px, do not fit the 343 px column, so the proposed row wraps inside its border – a new PR 4 requirement (README § 9.4). Accept it?

Drawn as shipped, not questions: --soft-foreground text is about 2.5:1 in light (known gap G-23, #344); the user bubble rounds one corner only, because rounded-2xl emits no rule (index.css:281).

The rhythm scale

Six named distances between blocks, all multiples of one spacing unit, so the density select on the bar scales them. The labels give Comfortable; Roomy is × 1.25, Compact × 0.875 and Compact for real × 0.75.

row2 · 8px
stack3 · 12px
list4 · 16px
inset5 · 20px
group6 · 24px
section8 · 32px

Between blocks a file uses a rhythm token; inside a control, the numeric scale; never a hand-typed pixel (README § 9.1). The Proposed view of every screen page is built from these six and the unit.
