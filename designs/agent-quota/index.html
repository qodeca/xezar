Agent quota – can my logins work now?

The cockpit half of #867 (step S4). A person supervising the leader wants to see what the leader sees: for every Claude Code and Codex login, how much of the short and weekly limits is used, when each resets, and whether the login can work right now. Today the cockpit shows none of it; a limit is learned when a task fails. This design adds a limits half to each account row in Settings → Agent accounts, one Plan limits block at the top of that pane, and a small chip visible on every page that answers the question in one glance. It only shows facts – it never switches a login, holds a task back or changes auto-resume.

The decisions, each with its reason

  • The limits live in the account rows people already open. A login’s limits are a fact about that login, next to its name and its sign-in state – not a second list of the same logins somewhere else. A dashed hairline separates “is it signed in” from “how much is left”, two different questions.
  • Every status is a sentence first. “Can work”, “Can work — near a limit: the 5-hour window is 92% used”, “Out until 17:10 CEST (+02:00) — the 5-hour limit is used up”, “Limits unknown — …”, “Limits not reported — …”. The dot (success, pending, danger, none) and the bar’s colour only reinforce the words.
  • Every number carries its age, and old numbers say so. “checked 6m ago”, “seen 3m ago in a running task”, “from a failed task 3m ago”; past 15 minutes the word “Stale” joins the age. A window read earlier than the rest says “as of 38m ago”. Nothing is ever shown as 0% because it is missing.
  • Refresh sits on the line it changes and shows its limit. Inside the 5-minute gap it stays focusable but unavailable, and the same line says when the next check is allowed. Refresh all states the pace once: 2 at a time, the rest wait.
  • The chip is a band above the sidebar footer, not a top bar. The cockpit has no desktop top bar, and the footer row must not wrap. On a phone the chip sits in the top bar’s status slot as “4/6 can work”. It is a button that opens a Popover naming the logins that are out or unknown, and links to the pane.
  • Hosted mode shows the limits, and still refuses account management. No folder, e-mail or Connected line; names, limits and Refresh only.
  • What is not reported says so. An API-key login reads “Limits not reported”; every field one tool never gives is listed under “Not reported by Claude Code” or “Not reported by Codex” in the row’s details.
