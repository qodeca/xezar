The sidebar in the mode, and the badge

There is no cockpit header in xezar, so the mode is shown where the cockpit already says what it is: the sidebar brand block, beside the version chip's own neighbourhood of facts. Two sidebars below, side by side, at their real 264 px width — the left one is today's cockpit with one registered project, the right one is the same cockpit in single-project mode.

Three differences, and two of them are subtractions: the mode badge on line two of the brand block, no Add project button, and no project groups — in the mode the flat nav is the only shape, whatever the registry says.

Why the badge is not on line one. The brand row is 264 px wide and already holds the tile, the wordmark and the repo/branch chip. Adding a fourth item truncates something, and the two candidates are the repo name — the answer to "which project am I in" — and the badge itself. Neither is a good thing to cut, so the badge takes its own line and nothing truncates. The mode adds about 20 px to the brand block; in global mode the block is byte-identical to today.

2. The badge, and the badge with the dev badge

The dev badge (data-slot="dev-badge", decisions.md D-08) sits on the brand tile's corner and means "this cockpit was built from source". The mode badge sits inline in the brand block and means "xezar's settings live in this folder". They answer different questions, so they keep different places and both are readable at once — which is the combination a reviewer will otherwise not see.
