CLI terminal design Overview Other projects

Other projects

With one cockpit per project, the sidebar and the command palette list the other registered projects as links to their own cockpit. Nothing opens in place. Specification: multi-instance.md § 6.

Sample: the cockpit for beta on port 4322. alpha runs on 4321, gamma is stopped, delta runs but its address is not known, and old-site’s folder is gone. Narrow the window to 375 px for the phone drawer: rows grow to 44 px and the actions wrap under the name.
